🐄 Cattle

Cattle provide milk, meat, and manure for crops.

  • Feed: Grass, hay, and clean water daily
  • Shelter: Shade from sun, dry place in rain
  • Health: Watch for coughing, thin body, or limping
  • Fencing: Strong posts — cattle are heavy

🐐 Goats

Goats are tough and useful on small farms.

  • Feed: Browse bushes, grass, and grain supplement
  • Milk: Milk twice daily if kept for dairy
  • Housing: Simple shed with ventilation
  • Breeding: Plan kidding season with good pasture

🐔 Chickens

Chickens give eggs and meat quickly on a small plot.

  • Coop: Dry floor, nesting boxes, predator-proof wire
  • Feed: Grain mix, kitchen scraps, grit for digestion
  • Water: Fresh water every day — hens drink a lot
  • Eggs: Collect daily; store in a cool dry place

🐷 Pigs

Pigs grow fast but need strong management.

  • Pen: Mud wallow optional; keep waste area separate
  • Feed: Balanced pig feed + vegetables — not only scraps
  • Health: Vaccinate and deworm on a schedule
  • Fencing: Bury wire — pigs root and push fences
