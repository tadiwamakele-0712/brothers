Monthly planting calendar

What to focus on each month:

Jan – Feb

Land prep, compost, repair tools and fences.

Mar – Apr

Plant maize and beans when rains start.

May – Jun

Weed crops, plant tomatoes, check irrigation.

Jul – Aug

Harvest early maize, plant wheat in cooler areas.

Sep – Oct

Store grain, dry cobs, prepare for next season.

Nov – Dec

Rest fields, plan rotation, maintain animal shelters.

🌽 Maize

  • Plant: Start of rainy season, 25 cm apart
  • Water: Critical at flowering — keep soil moist
  • Harvest: 3–4 months when cobs are dry
  • Tip: Add compost before planting for bigger cobs

🌾 Wheat

  • Plant: Cooler months, well-drained soil
  • Water: Moderate — avoid waterlogged fields
  • Harvest: When stalks turn golden brown
  • Tip: Rotate with legumes to restore soil nitrogen

🍅 Tomato

  • Plant: Seedlings after last frost / early rains
  • Water: Regular, at base — avoid wet leaves
  • Harvest: When fruit is firm and fully coloured
  • Tip: Stake plants early to support heavy fruit

🫘 Beans

  • Plant: Direct in rows, 10 cm apart
  • Water: Keep moist until pods form
  • Harvest: Pick when pods are full but still green
  • Tip: Great after maize — fixes nitrogen in soil

🌿 Sorghum

  • Plant: Warm season, drought-tolerant crop
  • Water: Less than maize — good for dry areas
  • Harvest: When grain heads are hard and dry
  • Tip: Useful when rainfall is low or unreliable
