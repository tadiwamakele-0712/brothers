About the Project

Tae Gift Hampers needed an online showcase for their curated gift boxes and hampers. Customers browse the collection, tap to view full-size photos, and enquire via form, WhatsApp, phone, or email — no e-commerce checkout, advertising and enquiries only.

  • Responsive product gallery with lightbox
  • Category filters (Wedding, Corporate, Birthday, etc.)
  • Dark / light theme toggle with localStorage
  • Contact form, WhatsApp, phone & email links
  • Social links — TikTok & Facebook
  • Mobile-friendly navigation
  • Deployed on GitHub Pages
