Import with confidence

From choosing the right vehicle abroad to clearing it locally — we stay with you at every stage.

  1. Step 1 — Order Choose the vehicle you want to purchase. Our car search engine will help you search through our inventory.
  2. Step 2 — Complete your order Set your purchase criteria such as the destination country, destination port, and so on.
  3. Step 3 — Make the payment Bank wire transfer.
  4. Step 4 — Shipment You can track your car’s shipment status through My Page > Estimate and Purchase. Your delivery information will be available for each order.
  5. Step 5 — Customs clearance Complete customs clearance. Each country can have different rules. You are responsible for checking the rules regarding the importation of the vehicles of your choice in your country.
