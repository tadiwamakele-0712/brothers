About Kunfre Enterprise

A trading name of Kunfre Hardware (Pvt) Ltd

Kunfre Hardware (Pvt) Ltd, trading as Kunfre Enterprise, is a registered Zimbabwean company and trusted supplier of industrial-grade sealing, fastening, electrical, instrumentation, and security products. Over 5+ years of operation, we have built a reputation as a dependable partner to the mining, oil & gas, construction, agriculture, water utilities, power generation, and general manufacturing sectors across Zimbabwe. Our engineers and product specialists work closely with clients to select the right solution for the most demanding environments.
