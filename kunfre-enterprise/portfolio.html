Who We Are

Kunfre Hardware (Pvt) Ltd, trading as Kunfre Enterprise, is a registered Zimbabwean company and trusted supplier of industrial-grade sealing, fastening, electrical, instrumentation, and security products. Over 5+ years of operation, we have built a reputation as a dependable partner to the mining, oil & gas, construction, agriculture, water utilities, power generation, and general manufacturing sectors across Zimbabwe.

What We Do

Our engineers and product specialists work closely with clients to select the right solution for the most demanding environments. From webbing tapes and gaskets to borehole installation, CCTV systems, and pump repairs — we supply products and deliver field services with ISO-aligned quality standards and Pan African and Asia coverage.
