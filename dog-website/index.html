Healthcare

Dog care checklist every pet parent needs

Happy dog, happy life — practical care habits we recommend for every companion in Mutare and beyond.

  1. 01

    Provide good nutrition

    Feed a balanced diet appropriate for your dog’s age, size & activity.

  2. 02

    Always fresh water

    Keep clean, fresh water available at all times.

  3. 03

    Daily exercise

    Keep your dog active with daily walks, play & mental stimulation.

  4. 04

    Groom regularly

    Brush coat, clean ears, trim nails & maintain hygiene.

  5. 05

    Health check-ups

    Schedule regular vet visits, keep vaccinations up to date & deworming.

  6. 06

    Train & socialize

    Use positive reinforcement to build good behavior & confidence.

  7. 07

    Show love & attention

    Spend quality time, be patient & build a strong bond.
