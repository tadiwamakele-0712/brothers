ZIMBABWEAN DANCEHALL FORCE JOKER DI GENIUS BRINGS FIFTEEN YEARS OF STAGE-READY AFROPOP ENERGY TO THE UK

Anesu Makele, known worldwide as Joker Di Genius, is a Zimbabwean-born dancehall and Afropop artist based in the United Kingdom. With more than fifteen years in the music industry, he delivers raw lyricism, riddim-driven performance, and collaboration-led releases built for clubs, festivals, and diaspora audiences.

ABOUT
Joker Di Genius has spent over a decade and a half refining a sound that bridges Zimbabwean street energy with international dancehall and Afropop. From early riddim features to standout collaborations, his catalogue spans hard-hitting singles, tribute records, gospel-tinged cuts, and high-energy stage anthems. Now based in the UK, he continues to write, record, and perform for audiences across Africa, Europe, and the global diaspora.
