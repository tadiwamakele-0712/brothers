Biography

About Anesu Makele

Anesu Makele — better known as Joker Di Genius — is a Zimbabwean dancehall and Afropop artist based in the United Kingdom.

For more than fifteen years he has built a catalogue defined by sharp lyricism, riddim culture, and collaborative firepower. From Zimbabwe’s dancehall circuit to UK stages and diaspora playlists, his sound moves between hard-edged street anthems, melodic Afropop hooks, and high-energy live performance.

Joker Di Genius works with producers and artists across the region, releasing singles that speak to hustle, love, faith, and street reality — music made to be felt in the room.

From Zimbabwe Roots to UK Stages: The Persistent Pulse of Joker Di Genius

In a scene that moves fast, Anesu Makele has lasted. Performing as Joker Di Genius, the Zimbabwean artist has spent fifteen-plus years sharpening a dancehall and Afropop identity that feels both local and export-ready — riddim-literate, collaborative, and built for the live room.

His catalogue tells the story: tributes and street talk, love records and faith cuts, features with voices like Honey Bee, Shellaz, Mazhambe Jnr, and King Mafaro. Now based in the United Kingdom, Joker Di Genius stands at a useful crossroads — African authenticity with UK accessibility — making him a natural booking for festivals, club nights, cultural showcases, and diaspora events.

What promoters get is experience. What audiences get is heat. That combination is the point of this press kit.
