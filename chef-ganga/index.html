Our story

Natties mouthwatering goodies

Cakes by Chef Ganga is a Harare bakery known for custom cakes, cupcake boxes, and celebration treats made to match your brief — colour, theme, and message included.

From birthday cakes and themed designs to elegant gold-and-white finishes, we bake what you imagine. Place your order from our WhatsApp catalog, chat with us about the look you want, and we deliver across Harare.
